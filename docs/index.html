What you need

A serialUPDI programmer, any will do, you can find our official adapter here ↗

Reading the roll

The ring lights one LED per point, clockwise from the top. A d20 needs more room than twelve lights allow, so the status LED marks +10: a natural 20 lights ten ring LEDs plus the status LED.

If the ring stays dark

A freshly inscribed chip is left halted in programming mode until it is reset. Unplug the UPDI adapter and pull and reseat the coin cell, the device is now ready for use.
